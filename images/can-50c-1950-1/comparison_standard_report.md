# 1950 Canada Silver 50 Cents (Half Dollar) - George VI - High Detail (XF/AU) - 80% Silver

## **Description**

For sale is a **1950 Canadian 50-Cent piece** featuring the portrait of King George VI. This coin is a significant year for collectors, featuring the intricate Coat of Arms reverse.

### **Key Features:**

- **Year:** 1950
- **Composition:** 80% Silver, 20% Copper
- **Silver Content:** Approximately 0.30 troy oz (ASW)
- **Diameter:** 29.72 mm
- **Design:** The obverse displays the head of King George VI (designed by T.H. Paget). The reverse features the Canadian Coat of Arms with supporters (designed by G.E. Kruger-Gray).
- **Variety Note:** 1950 halves are known for "Design in 0" and "No Design in 0" varieties. This example appears to be the standard **Design in 0** variety (texture visible inside the zero), though buyers are encouraged to verify via the high-resolution photos.

### **Condition Report:**

**Grade:** **XF/AU Details (Cleaned)**
